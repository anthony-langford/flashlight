### 2.41 Toronto CMA population estimate (July 1)

`statcan.toronto.population`

- **What it is:** Statistics Canada's estimate of the Toronto census metropolitan area population on July 1 each year. The data tags it with the January date of that year.
- **Why it matters for a buyer:** Population growth, especially from immigration and non-permanent residents, is a major driver of housing demand and rents.
- **How to read it:** Look at the year-over-year change. The estimates for 2024 and 2025 were both about 7.11 million, a sharp slowdown after growth of about 270 thousand the year before (checked 2026-10-14). ### 2.42 Toronto unemployment rate (three-month moving average, seasonally adjusted)

`statcan.toronto.unemployment_rate`

- **What it is:** The share of Toronto-area labour force looking for work without finding it. This table is a three-month moving average, so it is smoother and slower to turn than the raw monthly survey.
- **Why it matters for a buyer:** Job security is the foundation of a mortgage. A rising local unemployment rate weakens buyer confidence and demand.
- **How to read it:** Direction over several months matters more than the level. Sampling error is larger for a single city than for Canada overall.
